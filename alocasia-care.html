Alocasia care guide

Alocasia spp.

Dramatic, arrow-shaped leaves with bold veins.

DifficultySome experience
LightBright, indirect light
PetsToxic to pets
AttentionRegular, attentive care

⚠️ Keep away from pets. The Alocasia is toxic to cats and dogs if eaten. Place it out of reach, or choose a pet-friendly plant instead.

How often to water a Alocasia

Keep slightly moist, never soggy.

How much light does a Alocasia need?

Bright, indirect light.

Humidity

High.

Feeding

Monthly at half strength in spring and summer.

Common problems

It may go dormant in winter. Ease off watering and wait for spring.

Quick answers

Is the Alocasia safe for cats and dogs?

No. It is toxic to cats and dogs if eaten, so keep it out of reach. If your pet eats any plant and seems unwell, contact your vet.

Is the Alocasia good for beginners?

It suits someone with a little experience, as it is fussier about its conditions than most beginner plants.

Can a Alocasia grow in low light?

Not really. It needs bright, indirect light to stay healthy.
