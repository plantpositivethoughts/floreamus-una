Plant care guides

Seasonal tips · Winter

Helping plants through the cold months

  • Water less. Most houseplants grow slowly in winter, so let the soil dry out more between waterings.
  • Watch the radiators. Dry heat lowers humidity. Group plants together or use a pebble tray.
  • Chase the light. Move plants closer to windows, and wipe dust off leaves so they catch more light.
  • Check for pests. Spider mites love warm, dry rooms. Look under the leaves every couple of weeks.

Our story

Grown from a quiet moment

Floreamus Una began during the quiet of the 2020 lockdowns. Like many people, Brett found comfort in the natural world and a new passion for indoor plants.

What started as a hobby became a creative exploration: using architectural foliage and lush textures as design elements, until his home became a sanctuary of biophilic beauty.

Soon it was a family affair. Brett's partner Lydia and his sister Nicola shared the vision, and together they founded Floreamus Una, knowing that growing and nurturing plants is about far more than looks. It's good for the mind, too.
