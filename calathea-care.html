Calathea care guide

Calathea spp. (now Goeppertia)

Painted, patterned leaves that fold up at night, like its relative the prayer plant.

DifficultySome experience
LightLow light, bright, indirect light
PetsPet-friendly
AttentionRegular, attentive care

🐾 Pet-friendly. The Calathea is considered non-toxic to cats and dogs, making it a good choice for homes with pets.

How often to water a Calathea

Keep the soil lightly moist but never soggy. Use rainwater or filtered water if you can.

How much light does a Calathea need?

Medium, indirect light. Direct sun fades and scorches the patterns.

Humidity

High. Kitchens and bathrooms with a window suit them.

Feeding

Half-strength feed monthly from spring to late summer.

Common problems

Crispy edges mean dry air; curling leaves mean thirst.

Quick answers

Is the Calathea safe for cats and dogs?

Yes. It is considered non-toxic to cats and dogs.

Is the Calathea good for beginners?

It suits someone with a little experience, as it is fussier about its conditions than most beginner plants.

Can a Calathea grow in low light?

Yes, it tolerates lower light, though it will grow more slowly.
