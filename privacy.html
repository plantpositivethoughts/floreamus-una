Privacy

We keep things simple: this website collects as little about you as possible. Here's exactly what happens when you use it.

Who we are

This website is run by Floreamus Una. If you have any questions about your information, please get in touch.

Browsing the site

The site doesn't use cookies or adverts. To understand which pages are useful, we count visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly service that doesn't use cookies or collect personal information, and can't identify you. It's hosted by GitHub Pages, and like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address to keep the service running and secure. Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which also receives your IP address when the page loads. You can read GitHub's privacy statement and Google's privacy policy.

The Weekly Bloom newsletter

Our newsletter is run on Substack. If you subscribe, you do so on Substack's website and they hold your email address under their privacy policy. You can unsubscribe at any time from any email.

Contacting us

If you message us on social media, or through our contact form once it's available, we use your name, contact details and message only to reply to you. Messages sent through the form will be delivered to us by email using a form service. We don't add you to any mailing list or share your details with anyone else, and we delete messages once they're no longer needed.

The Bookshelf discussions

Book discussions use giscus, which stores comments on GitHub. To post, you sign in with a GitHub account, and your username and comments are publicly visible. GitHub's privacy statement applies.

Social media and other sites

Links to TikTok, Instagram, Substack, Amazon and our app take you to those services, and their own privacy policies apply there.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete any personal information we hold about you. Just contact us. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
