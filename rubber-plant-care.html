Rubber Plant care guide

Ficus elastica

Big, glossy, deep-green or burgundy leaves on a sturdy stem.

DifficultyEasy
LightBright, indirect light, some direct sun
PetsToxic to pets
AttentionA little regular care

⚠️ Keep away from pets. The Rubber Plant is toxic to cats and dogs if eaten. Place it out of reach, or choose a pet-friendly plant instead.

How often to water a Rubber Plant

When the top 5 cm of soil are dry.

How much light does a Rubber Plant need?

Bright, indirect light.

Humidity

Average.

Feeding

Monthly in spring and summer.

Common problems

Wipe dust off the leaves to keep them shining.

Quick answers

Is the Rubber Plant safe for cats and dogs?

No. It is toxic to cats and dogs if eaten, so keep it out of reach. If your pet eats any plant and seems unwell, contact your vet.

Is the Rubber Plant good for beginners?

Yes, it is one of the more forgiving houseplants and copes well with the odd missed watering.

Can a Rubber Plant grow in low light?

Not really. It needs bright, indirect light, some direct sun to stay healthy.
