Flourish Together

A story about kindness, new beginnings and growing together

By Brett Westwood & Floreamus Una

What if learning to care for a plant could help a child discover how to care for themselves, too? Available in paperback.

Get it on Amazon

A little story about growing, caring and believing in yourself

Little seeds need care to grow, and so do we!

What if learning to care for a plant could help a child discover how to care for themselves, too?

Join Lydia on a heartwarming journey of growth, self-discovery and learning that, just like a little plant, we all need time, patience, kindness and care to flourish.

Through Lydia's experiences, young readers will discover that it's okay to have difficult days, that asking a trusted grown-up for help is a sign of strength, and that growing doesn't mean being perfect. Sometimes, the most beautiful things grow in their own time.

But the adventure doesn't end with the story!

Inside this special book, you'll discover:

📖

A heartwarming story

That encourages children to nurture themselves as well as the world around them.

🪴

Simple plant-care activities

That introduce children to responsibility, patience and the joy of watching something grow.

✅

Healthy-habit checklists

To help little ones develop positive everyday routines.

🌷

A feelings garden

Where children can begin to recognise, explore and express their emotions.

📓

A growing journal

That encourages reflection, creativity and celebrating small steps forward.

Perfect for sharing at home, in the classroom or at bedtime, Let's Flourish Together offers a gentle way for adults and children to talk about feelings, build healthy habits and explore the importance of looking after themselves.

Whether your little one is learning to navigate big feelings, discovering the wonders of nature or simply finding their own way in the world, this book offers a reassuring reminder:

You don't have to grow all at once. You just need a little care, a little patience and room to bloom.

Let's plant the seeds of kindness, confidence and wellbeing, and flourish together! 🌱
