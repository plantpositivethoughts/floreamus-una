Parlour Palm care guide

Chamaedorea elegans

A graceful, slow-growing palm that has brightened British homes since Victorian times.

DifficultyEasy
LightLow light, bright, indirect light
PetsPet-friendly
AttentionA little regular care

🐾 Pet-friendly. The Parlour Palm is considered non-toxic to cats and dogs, making it a good choice for homes with pets.

How often to water a Parlour Palm

When the top few centimetres are dry. Water less in winter.

How much light does a Parlour Palm need?

Low to medium, indirect light.

Humidity

Average; enjoys a little extra.

Feeding

Monthly in spring and summer.

Common problems

Brown leaf tips mean dry air or uneven watering.

Quick answers

Is the Parlour Palm safe for cats and dogs?

Yes. It is considered non-toxic to cats and dogs.

Is the Parlour Palm good for beginners?

Yes, it is one of the more forgiving houseplants and copes well with the odd missed watering.

Can a Parlour Palm grow in low light?

Yes, it tolerates lower light, though it will grow more slowly.
